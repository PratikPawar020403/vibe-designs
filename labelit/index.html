03 Methodology

The Five-Step
Pipeline

A calibrated sequence guaranteeing zero air entrapment, microscopic edge alignment, and durable finish.

Compatible Substrates
Borosilicate Glass Recycled Flint Anodized Aluminium Bio-Resins Matte Ceramic
ISO 9001 Certified

  1. Deionization and corona flame surface treatment eliminate static and optimize glass surface energy.

    Tolerance: 0.00% Contaminant

  2. Multi-point laser registration cameras identify vessel seams and position target axes within ±0.1mm.

    Laser Optical Scan

  3. Dynamic pneumatic wipe-down rollers apply progressive pressure to purge air and establish uniform adhesion.

    Dynamic Wipe Rollers

  4. 360-degree machine vision cameras verify barcode grade, margin parallelism, and micro-emboss integrity.

    100% Vision Verification

  5. Robotic collation and padded cellular crating protect labeled vessels during transit to bottling lines worldwide.

    Global White-Glove Transit
